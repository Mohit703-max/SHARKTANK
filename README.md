# 🚀 VERA

A full-stack, proof-based habit and fitness tracking application designed to eliminate self-reporting bias through verifiable challenges.

## 🎯 Vision & Core Concept
Traditional habit trackers rely on the honor system—letting users blindly click "Done" even when they didn't do the work. **Vera** fixes this by requiring verifiable proof and cryptographic/dynamic challenges to validate consistency before logging scores or streaks.
